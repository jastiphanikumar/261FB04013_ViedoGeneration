# 🎬 AI Product Advertisement Collection

A collection of AI-generated cinematic product advertisements created using **Google Flow**. This project showcases two creative advertising concepts: a satisfying chocolate commercial and a premium NOVA X smartwatch launch advertisement.

The goal is to demonstrate how AI video generation can create professional-looking product commercials with cinematic visuals, storytelling, voiceovers, background music, and sound effects.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project features two unique advertisement concepts:

### 🍫 1. Chocolate Pouring Advertisement

A visually satisfying, cinematic chocolate commercial focused on rich textures, smooth chocolate pouring, and delicious close-up shots.

**Key Highlights:**
- Smooth, flowing melted chocolate.
- Extreme macro product photography.
- Rich chocolate textures and glossy reflections.
- Slow-motion pouring effects.
- Cinematic lighting and transitions.
- Satisfying sound effects and background music.

**Visual Style:** Premium food commercial, photorealistic macro cinematography, warm chocolate tones.

### ⌚ 2. NOVA X – Beyond Time

A 15-second ultra-premium cinematic advertisement for a fictional luxury smartwatch brand called **NOVA X**.

**Product Features:**
- Sleek black titanium body.
- Brilliant AMOLED display.
- Elegant rotating crown.
- Premium black silicone strap.
- Futuristic and sophisticated design.

**Advertisement Scenes:**

| Scene | Duration | Description |
|---|---|---|
| The Hook | 0–3 sec | Smartwatch emerges from darkness with blue lighting |
| Product Reveal | 3–6 sec | Cinematic 360-degree product showcase |
| Feature Showcase | 6–10 sec | AMOLED display and fitness interface |
| Lifestyle Moment | 10–13 sec | Professional wearing the smartwatch |
| Brand Reveal | 13–15 sec | NOVA X logo and Beyond Time tagline |

**Audio Features:**
- Deep male English voiceover.
- Cinematic electronic background music.
- Powerful bass impacts.
- Futuristic transition sounds.
- Synchronized metallic sound effects.
